The service

ACS collects and presents anti-cheat evidence for Counter-Strike 1.6. It inspects a running game process, compares what it finds against signatures and an artifact corpus, and stores the result as a report. It is an evidence tool, not a policing system: it does not ban, kick or act on a player by itself.
